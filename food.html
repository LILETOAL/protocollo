Burritos · the missed-meal answer

A whole meal in one piece, vacuum sealed one by one, no equipment to reheat and no decisions to make. Four variants so the freezer never holds one flavour.

VariantPer unitFreezesWhy it is on the list
Beef & potatothe original 150 g lean beef mince · 100 g potato · 1.5 eggs · 10 g cottage cheese · 8 g tomato paste · 10 ml Worcestershire · 1 large tortilla · 2.5 g sriracha mayo good Highest protein of the four. Potato is the weak point: it only survives the freezer if it went in browned and dry.
Pulled chicken, rice & black bean 160 g chicken thigh · 40 g dry rice · 40 g black beans · 1 egg · 10 g cottage cheese · 1 large tortilla · smoked paprika, cumin best Rice freezes and reheats better than any potato. Beans add the fibre the beef version lacks.
Turkey & sweet potato, chipotle 150 g turkey mince · 110 g sweet potato · 1.5 eggs · 10 g cottage cheese · 5 g olive oil · 1 large tortilla · chipotle good The leanest, which is why it carries added oil. Sweet potato holds up better than white.
Lentil, mushroom & ricemeatless 50 g dry lentils · 80 g mushrooms · 40 g dry rice · 1.5 eggs · 15 g cottage cheese · 1 large tortilla best Cheapest, highest fibre, and the one that stops four beef weeks in a row. Lowest protein, so pair it with the shake.

Three techniques that decide whether they survive the freezer, and they apply to all four. Brown the potato or rice properly dry, never soft and steamed. Scramble the eggs soft, pull them off the heat before they look done, and fold in the blended cottage cheese, because that dairy is what stops frozen egg weeping on thaw. Cool the filling completely before rolling, or condensation inside the vacuum bag falls the tortilla apart.

Reheat: 20 minutes at 180 °C from frozen, or 3 minutes a side in a dry pan once thawed. Never a microwave. And never eaten alone: they are protein dense and carb light, so they need oats and a banana beside them.

Vacuum sealing does not extend fridge life. Removing oxygen selects for different bacteria, it does not remove them. Four days, then freezer or bin. In the freezer it is a real gain: one to two months becomes three to six. Never on liquids, they get pulled into the machine, so freeze the sauce solid first and seal the block.
